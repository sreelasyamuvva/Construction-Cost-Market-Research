# Construction Cost Estimation & Budget Management

### Market Research & Validation | Real Estate & Construction

## 📌 Overview

This project explores the challenges homeowners face when estimating and managing the budget for constructing a new house.

Construction costs can vary during a project due to changes in material prices, labour costs, contractor charges, design changes, and unexpected expenses. These factors can make it difficult for homeowners to stay within their planned budget.

The project uses primary survey research and secondary data analysis to understand the extent of the problem and identify opportunities for better construction cost estimation and budget management.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

> Homeowners planning to construct a new house often struggle to accurately estimate and control construction costs, leading to unexpected expenses and budget overruns.

---

## 🔍 Research Objectives

- Validate whether construction cost overruns are a recurring experience.
- Identify the major sources of differences between estimated and actual costs.
- Understand how homeowners currently estimate and track construction expenses.
- Assess the need for a more reliable construction cost estimation and budget management solution.

---

## 📊 Research Methodology

### Primary Research

A survey was conducted among people who had constructed, were constructing, or were planning to construct a new house.

The survey explored:

- Initial construction budget
- Budget estimation methods
- Confidence in initial estimates
- Difference between estimated and final costs
- Causes of cost differences
- Contractor quotation comparison
- Expense tracking methods
- Budget management challenges
- Interest in a better estimation solution

### Secondary Research

A 100-record construction analysis dataset was used to perform additional quantitative analysis and identify cost patterns.

---

## 📈 Key Findings

The analysis indicated a clear problem signal around construction budget management.

- **93%** of the analysed records reported some level of cost overrun.
- **55%** reported a final cost up to 10% higher than the initial estimate.
- **28%** reported a 10–20% increase.
- **10%** reported an increase above 20%.
- **87%** indicated a likelihood of using a reliable construction cost estimator.

These findings were used to understand the prevalence and severity of construction budget differences and the potential demand for better estimation support.
